NZ Planning System v8.4

Changes from v8.3:
- Standard page and browse headers now use the same title scale as the homepage while retaining the existing inner-page search styling.
- Added direct header links to the official Planning Act 2026 and Natural Environment Act 2026 legislation pages.
- Recently visited now records searches as well as pages, and updates immediately after a search.
- Reference-list links now open the referring page at the first highlighted mention of the referenced page title; existing block anchors are retained.
- Search-result page links highlight each search term and automatically scroll to the first match.

The Funnel Folly header is intentionally unchanged.
