§99 — How land use plan is prepared or changed

PagePA2026provision
  • (1) Schedule 3 has effect according to its terms.
  • (2) A territorial authority must prepare or change the land use plan for its district in accordance with Parts 1, 2, and 4 of Schedule 3.
  • (3) A regional council must prepare or change the land use plan for the coastal marine area within its region in accordance with Parts 1, 2, and 4 of Sched- ule 3.
  • (4) Any person (other than the local authority that prepared the plan) may request a change to a land use plan in accordance with Part 2 of Schedule 3.
  • (5) Part 3 of Schedule 3 sets out provisions relating to when rules in a proposed land use plan have legal effect.
