§221 — Statutory officer

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
    • (1) The chief executive must appoint a person to be the statutory officer.
    • (2) The chief executive must be satisfied that the person has the knowledge, skills, and experience to perform and exercise the functions, powers, and duties of the statutory officer.
    • (3) The functions, powers, and duties of the statutory officer are set out in Sched- ule 11. Schedule 11 has effect according to its terms.
    • (4) The person must be employed in the Ministry but need not be a current employee of the Ministry at the time of appointment.
    • (5) When performing or exercising a function, power, or duty under this Act, the statutory officer must act independently of the Minister and the chief executive.
    • (6) The statutory officer must perform or exercise a function, power, or duty under this Act in accordance with any requirements in a national instrument.
