§246 — Chief executive’s system performance function

PagePA2026provision
    • (1) The chief executive has a function to maintain strategic oversight of the system established under this Act and the Natural Environment Act 2026.
    • (2) The chief executive must perform that function, in accordance with the require- ments of this subpart, by—
      • (a) monitoring and reporting on—
      • (i) the efficiency and effectiveness of that system; and
        • (ii) the extent to which the goals of those Acts are achieved; and
      • (b) identifying and responding to system-wide issues.
