§70 — Amendments to national policy direction without full process

PagePA2026provision

  • (1) The Minister may recommend the making of an amendment to a national pol- icy direction without complying with section 65 if the amendment is needed to make changes that are no more than minor in effect, to correct errors, or to make similar technical alterations.
  • (2) Section 66 does not apply to amendments recommended under this section.
