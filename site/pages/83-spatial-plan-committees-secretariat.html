§83 — Spatial plan committee’s secretariat

PagePA2026provision
  • (1) A spatial plan committee must appoint a secretariat to provide it with technical and administrative support.
  • (2) A secretariat must include 1 or more technical experts or advisors who are appointed by the chief executive of the Ministry and whose work for the secre- tariat is funded by the Crown.
  • (3) The secretariat, including a member appointed by the chief executive, is sub- ject to the direction of the spatial plan committee.
