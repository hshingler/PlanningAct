§4 — Purpose

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
  • The purpose of this Act is to provide for the enjoyment of land by establishing a framework for planning and regulating the use and development of land.
