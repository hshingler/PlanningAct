§80 — Overview of effect of regional spatial plans

PagePA2026provision
  • (1) A regional spatial plan has effect under the following legislation as provided for in that legislation: This Act
    • (a) a land use plan under this Act must implement the regional spatial plan of the relevant region (see section 103(2)(a) of this Act): Natural Environment Act 2026
    • (b) a natural environment plan under the Natural Environment Act 2026 must implement the regional spatial plan of the region (see section 106(2)(a)(i) of that Act):
  • Land Transport Management Act 2003
    • (c) a regional land transport plan under the Land Transport Management Act 2003 must be consistent with the regional spatial plan of the region (see section 14(a)(iii) of that Act):
    • (d) the Minister responsible for administering the Land Transport Manage- ment Act 2003 must take into account any relevant regional spatial plan when preparing or reviewing a government policy statement on land transport (see section 67(1)(b)(iv) of that Act): Local Government Act 2002
    • (e) a long-term plan under the Local Government Act 2002 must set out steps to implement or progress the actions for which the local authority is responsible under this subpart (see clause 26A of Schedule 10 of that Act).
  • (2) This section is a guide only to the effect of key legislation that provides for the effect of regional spatial plans.
