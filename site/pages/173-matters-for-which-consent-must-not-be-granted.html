§173 — Matters for which consent must not be granted

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
  • A consent authority must not grant a planning consent if—
    • (a) it is contrary to—
      • (i) any regulations:
      • (ii) a wāhi tapu condition included in a customary marine title order or agreement:
      • (iii) section 55(2) of the Marine and Coastal Area (Takutai Moana) Act 2011:
      • (iv) prohibitions or restrictions that apply in relation to a wāhi tapu or wāhi tapu area recognised under subpart 2 of Part 4 of the Ngā Rohe Moana o Ngā Hapū o Ngāti Porou Act 2019:
    • (b) it should have been notified and was not.
