§96 — Regional spatial plan must not apply to certain islands

PagePA2026provisionRSP
  • A regional spatial plan must not apply to—
    • (a) the islands referred to in section 213 of this Act (for which the Minister of Conservation has certain powers of a local authority); or
    • (b) the coastal marine area surrounding those islands (for which the Minister of Conservation has certain powers of a regional council under section 219 of the Natural Environment Act 2026).
