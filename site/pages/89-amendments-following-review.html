§89 — Amendments following review

PagePA2026provision
  • If, after completing a review, a spatial plan committee recommends that a regional spatial plan be amended, the local authorities of the region must, as soon as is reasonably practicable, initiate a change to the regional spatial plan in accordance with [Schedule 2]([[Schedule 2 — Regional spatial plans]]) (see clause 3 of that schedule).
