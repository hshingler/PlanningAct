§223 — Obligations relating to statutory acknowledgements

PagePA2026provision
  • A territorial authority must ensure that, in undertaking its planning and other functions, powers, and duties under this subpart, its ability to fulfil the obliga- tions relating to any statutory acknowledgements applying within, or in any part of, its district is not impeded.
