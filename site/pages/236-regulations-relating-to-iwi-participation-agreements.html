§236 — Regulations relating to iwi participation agreements

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
    • (1) The Governor-General may, by Order in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister, make regulations relating to iwi participation agreements for all or any of the following purposes:
      • (a) specifying content that may be included in, and content that must not be included in, an iwi participation agreement:
      • (b) prescribing the form of iwi participation agreements:
      • (c) prescribing the process for initiating and reaching agreement on an iwi participation agreement, including recommended time frames for responding to requests for an iwi participation agreement and concluding discussions:
      • (d) providing dispute resolution processes for resolving disputes that arise in the course of negotiating an iwi participation agreement:
      • (e) prescribing or providing for any other matters that relate to iwi participa- tion agreements.
    • (2) Regulations made under this section are secondary legislation (see Part 3 of the Legislation Act 2019 for publication requirements).
    • Legislation Act 2019 requirements for secondary legislation made under this section
    • Publication PCO must publish it on the legislation website and notify LA19 s 69(1)(c) it in the Gazette
    • Presentation The Minister must present it to the House of LA19 s 114 Representatives
    • Disallowance It may be disallowed by the House of Representatives LA19 ss 115, 116 This note is not part of the Act.
