§73 — Purpose of regional spatial plan

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
  • The purpose of a regional spatial plan is to set the strategic direction for growth and change in a region for a time frame of 30 years or longer.
