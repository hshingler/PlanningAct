§190 — Duration of planning consent

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
    • (1) The period for which a planning consent is granted is unlimited, unless other- wise specified in the consent.
    • (2) This section is subject to section 194 (lapsing of consents).
