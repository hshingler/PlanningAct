§218 — Functions of territorial authorities

PagePA2026provision
  • A territorial authority has the following functions:
    • (a) making and reviewing a land use plan for its district:
    • (b) administering and implementing its land use plan:
    • (c) monitoring the implementation of this Act within its district:
    • (d) acting as a consent authority for its district:
    • (e) monitoring and enforcing compliance with this Act:
    • (f) jointly with other local authorities, making and maintaining a regional spatial plan for the region:
    • (g) regulating and managing effects on the built environment:
    • (h) any other functions, powers, or duties specified in this Act or in the Nat- ural Environment Act 2026.
