§147 — Time limit for public notification or targeted notification

PagePA2026provision
  • A consent authority must, within 20 working days after the application is lodged,—
    • (a) decide whether to give public or targeted notification of an application for a planning consent in accordance with sections 148 and 149; and
    • (b) notify the applicant, in the manner prescribed in regulations, if it decides to do so.
