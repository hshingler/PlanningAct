§123 — Disputes relating to whether land use plan implements national instruments or regional spatial plan

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
  • (1) This section applies if there is a dispute about whether a land use plan imple- ments—
    • (a) a national instrument; or
    • (b) the regional spatial plan.
  • (2) The Minister, the local authority responsible for the land use plan, or the spatial plan committee responsible for the regional spatial plan may refer the dispute to the Environment Court.
  • (3) If the Environment Court considers that the land use plan does not implement the national instrument or regional spatial plan, the court must order the local authority to amend the land use plan in accordance with section 61(1) or (2) (as applicable).
  • (4) However, the Environment Court does not need to make an order if it considers that the departure from the national instrument or the regional spatial plan is minor or inconsequential.
