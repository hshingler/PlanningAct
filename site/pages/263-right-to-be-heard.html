§263 — Right to be heard

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
  • Except as provided in section 265 (which relates to interim enforcement orders), before deciding an application for an enforcement order, the Environ- ment Court must—
    • (a) hear the applicant; and
    • (b) hear any person against whom the order is sought who wishes to be heard, but only if that person notifies the Registrar that they wish to be heard within 15 working days after the date on which they were notified of the application.
