NEA §329 — Conditions to be satisfied before regulations made under section 328

PageprovisionNEA2026
  • (1) Before recommending the making of regulations under section 328, the Minister must—
    • (a) follow the process set out in section 92 (other than section 92(3)﻿(a)﻿(ii) and (iii)﻿(B) and (C)) as if the recommendation for regulations were for a proposed national instrument; and
    • (b) comply with subsection (2) or (3) as applicable; and
    • (c) for a recommendation for regulations under section 328(2)﻿(b), identify the domain (as defined in section 47) to which the levy applies.
  • (2) For regulations recommended under section 328(2)﻿(a), the Minister must ensure that the rates are set so that the levy does not exceed the anticipated costs of any of the following activities that have been included in a natural environment plan and are undertaken for the purposes set out in section 328(2)﻿(a):
    NEP
    • (a) restoring ecosystems:
    • (b) reducing the environmental impact of the use of the resource:
    • (c) providing solutions (including research and development) to—
      • (i) manage increased demand for the resource; and
      • (ii) enable more efficient use of the resource; and
    • (d) activities that increase the availability of the resource.
  • (3) For regulations recommended under section 328(2)﻿(b), the Minister must, after considering information provided by local authorities or relevant central government agencies relating to the costs of the following activities, ensure that the rate does not exceed the costs of those activities:
    • (a) system performance monitoring, including associated data collection:
    • (b) environmental monitoring and associated data collection:
    • (c) development and review of national standards:
    • (d) research, including modelling and tool development, to support any of the activities in this subsection:
    • (e) enabling the trade and transfer of natural resource permits to be operationalised:
    • (f) spatial and regulatory planning in the coastal marine area.
  • (4) A regional council that is directed or empowered to set levies under regulations made under section 328(3)﻿(j) must do so in accordance with subsection (2) or (3), as applicable, as if it were the Minister.
  • (5) In determining the amount of the levy or a method for calculating the amount under regulations recommended under section 328(2)﻿(b), the Minister or regional council must have regard to the extent that the benefit of the activities is obtained by those persons paying the levy compared to the wider community.
