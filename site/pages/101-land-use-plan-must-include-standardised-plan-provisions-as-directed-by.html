§101 — Land use plan must include standardised plan provisions as directed by

PagePA2026provision
  • (1) A local authority—
    • (a) must include a standardised plan provision in its land use plan or a pro- posed land use plan if national standards direct it to include that provi- sion; and
    • (b) may include a standardised plan provision in its land use plan or a pro- posed land use plan if national standards direct that it may include that provision.
  • (2) A local authority may do any of the following only if authorised by national standards:
    • (a) determine where a standardised plan provision applies spatially:
    • (b) select which standardised plan provision, out of 2 or more alternatives set out in the national standards, it will include in its plan:
    • (c) determine any content in a standardised plan provision from within parameters set out in the national standards:
    • (d) choose not to include a standardised plan provision.
  • (3) A local authority must not amend a standardised plan provision.
