§2 — Commencement

PagePA2026provision
    • (1) This Act comes into force on the day after Royal assent, with some exceptions.
    • (2) The following provisions come into force 1 month after Royal assent:
      • (a) section 343(2) (amendments to the Resource Management Act 1991 set out in Part 2 of Schedule 12):
      • (b) section 343(3) (amendments to the Fast-track Approvals Act 2024 set out in Part 3 of Schedule 12).
    • (3) The following provisions come into force on a date or dates set by Order in Council:
      • (a) sections 20 to 24 and 26 to 33 (provisions relating to duties and restric- tions):
      • (b) section 38 (consequences of certain activity classifications):
      • (c) section 99(1) but only for the purpose of giving effect to Part 3 of Sched- ule 3 (provisions relating to legal effect of rules in proposed plans):
      • (d) sections 118, 119, 124, 125, and 126(3) (provisions relating to land use plans):
      • (e) section 129(1) but only for the purpose of giving effect to clauses 4 to 6, 13, and 47 to 56 of Schedule 5 (provisions relating to designations):
      • (f) subparts 2 to 6 of Part 4 (provisions relating to planning consents, including Schedule 6) and sections 207 to 210 (provisions relating to permitted activities and existing use certificates):
      • (g) section 211(3) but only for the purpose of giving effect to clauses 5 to 78 of Schedule 7 (provisions relating to subdivision and reclamation):
      • (h) sections 259 to 314 (provisions relating to enforcement, including Schedule 8):
      • (i) sections 315 to 319 (provisions relating to emergency works):
      • (j) section 343(5) (amendments to Acts specified in Part 5 of Schedule 12).
    • (4) Sections 221 and 222 come into force on a date set by Order in Council (provi- sions relating to the statutory officer, including Schedule 11).
    • (5) Section 343(4) comes into force on a date set by Order in Council (amend- ments to the Resource Management Act 1991 relating to the Planning Tribunal set out in Part 4 of Schedule 12).
    • (6) Section 343(6) comes into force on a date set by Order in Council (amend- ments to Acts specified in Part 6 of Schedule 12).
    • (7) Section 344 comes into force on a date set by Order in Council (repeal of the Resource Management Act 1991).
    • (8) Any part of this Act that has not come into force 4 years after Royal assent comes into force then.
    • (9) An Order in Council made under this section is secondary legislation (see Part 3 of the Legislation Act 2019 for publication requirements).
    • Legislation Act 2019 requirements for secondary legislation made under this section
    • Publication PCO must publish it on the legislation website and notify LA19 s 69(1)(c) it in the Gazette
    • Presentation The Minister must present it to the House of LA19 s 114 Representatives
    • Disallowance It may be disallowed by the House of Representatives LA19 ss 115, 116 This note is not part of the Act.
