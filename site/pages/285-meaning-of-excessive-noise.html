§285 — Meaning of excessive noise

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
    • (1) In this Act, excessive noise means any noise that is under human control and of such a nature as to unreasonably interfere with the peace, comfort, and con- venience of any person (other than a person in or at the place from which the noise is being emitted), but does not include any noise emitted by any—
      • (a) aircraft being operated during, or immediately before or after, flight; or
      • (b) vehicle being driven on a road (within the meaning of section 2(1) of the Land Transport Act 1998); or
      • (c) train, other than when being tested (when stationary), maintained, loa- ded, or unloaded.
    • (2) Without limiting subsection (1), excessive noise—
      • (a) includes noise that exceeds a standard for noise prescribed by a national rule; and
      • (b) may include noise emitted by—
      • (i) a musical instrument; or
        • (ii) an electrical appliance; or
        • (iii) a machine, however powered; or
        • (iv) a person or group of persons; or
      • (v) an explosion or vibration.
