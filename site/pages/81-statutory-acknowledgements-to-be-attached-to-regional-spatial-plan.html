§81 — Statutory acknowledgements to be attached to regional spatial plan

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
  • (1) Every statutory acknowledgement that applies in a region must be attached to the regional spatial plan for that region.
  • (2) The provisions of the legislation that provide for a statutory acknowledgement apply.
  • (3) The attachment of a statutory acknowledgement to a regional spatial plan is for public information only and, unless adopted by the local authorities of the region as part of the regional spatial plan, is not part of the regional spatial plan.
