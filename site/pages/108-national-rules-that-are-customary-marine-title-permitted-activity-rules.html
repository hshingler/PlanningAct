§108 — National rules that are customary marine title permitted activity rules

PagePA2026provision
  • (1) This section applies if a national rule that classifies an activity as a permitted activity is to apply to any part of the common marine and coastal area.
  • (2) The local authority must consider whether the activity (as permitted by the national rule) will or is likely to have—
    • (a) an adverse effect that is more than minor on the relationship of a custom- ary marine title group with their customary marine title area; or
    • (b) an adverse effect that is more than minor on the relationship of an appli- cant group with the area for which they seek customary marine title.
  • (3) If the local authority considers that the activity will or is likely to have an adverse effect described in subsection (2)(a) or (b), the local authority must amend its land use plan, and any proposed land use plan, to identify the national rule as a customary marine title permitted activity rule.
  • (4) The local authority must make the amendments—
    • (a) so that they come into force on, or as soon as is reasonably practicable after, the date on which the national rule comes into force; and
    • (b) without using the process in Schedule 3.
