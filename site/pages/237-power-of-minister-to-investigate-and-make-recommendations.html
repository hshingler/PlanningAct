§237 — Power of Minister to investigate and make recommendations

PagePA2026provision
    • (1) The Minister may—
      • (a) investigate the exercise or performance by a local authority of any of its functions, powers, or duties under this Act; and
      • (b) make recommendations to the local authority on its exercise or perform- ance of those functions, powers, or duties; and
      • (c) investigate the failure or omission by a local authority to exercise or per- form any of its functions, powers, or duties under this Act; and
      • (d) make recommendations to the local authority on its failure or omission to exercise or perform those functions, powers, or duties.
    • (2) The Minister may—
      • (a) specify a time frame within which a local authority must respond to a recommendation and a format for that response; and
      • (b) require a local authority to respond within the specified time frame.
