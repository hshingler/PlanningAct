§317 — Planning consents for emergency works

PagePA2026provision
  • (1) If an activity is undertaken under section 315 or 316, the relevant authority that authorised the activity must notify the appropriate consent authority, within 7 working days, that the activity has been undertaken.
  • (2) If the activity, but for section 315 or 316, would contravene section 21 or 22 and the adverse effects of the activity continue, then the relevant authority that authorised the activity must, within 30 working days of the notification under subsection (1), apply to the appropriate consent authority for any planning con- sents required for the activity.
  • (3) If the application is made within the time stated in subsection (2), the activity may continue until the application is decided and any appeals have been finally determined.
  • (4) In this section, relevant authority has the meaning given in section 315.
