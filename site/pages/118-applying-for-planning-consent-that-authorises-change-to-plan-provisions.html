§118 — Applying for planning consent that authorises change to plan provisions

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
  • (1) A person may apply under section 132 for a planning consent that authorises a change to the provisions in a land use plan that apply to an area under section 119.
  • (2) A plan may be changed under section 119 only if the change—
    • (a) involves the application of standardised plan provisions to the area; and
    • (b) does not include any bespoke provisions.
  • (3) See also section 169.
  • (4) For the purposes of this section and section 119, a subdivision consent is given effect to if the territorial authority has issued a certificate under clause 23 of Schedule 7 (certificate that consent conditions are complied with) for that con- sent.
