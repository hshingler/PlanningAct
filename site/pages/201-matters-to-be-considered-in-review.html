§201 — Matters to be considered in review

PagePA2026provision
  • When reviewing the conditions of a planning consent, the consent authority—
    • (a) must have regard to the matters specified in subpart 4 and to whether the activity allowed by the consent will continue to be viable after the change; and
    • (b) in the case of a review under section 198(2), must have regard to any reasons that the court provided for making the order requiring the review; and
    • (c) may have regard to the manner in which the consent has been used.
