§131 — Prior consultation not required

PagePA2026provision
  • The following applies to an applicant for a planning consent and the consent authority:
    • (a) neither has a duty under this Act to consult any person about the applica- tion; and
    • (b) each must comply with a duty under any other legislation to consult any person about the application; and
    • (c) each may consult any person about the application.
