§192 — Applicant may lodge new application that complies with water services

PagePA2026provision
  • standard
    • (1) A consent holder that is operating under section 191 may—
      • (a) withdraw their application for a new consent; and
      • (b) prepare a new application that complies with a relevant water services standard.
    • (2) Despite the consent holder withdrawing their application,—
      • (a) they may continue to operate under section 191; but
      • (b) they must submit the new application within 6 months after the date that the application is withdrawn.
