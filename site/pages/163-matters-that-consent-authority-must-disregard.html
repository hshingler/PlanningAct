§163 — Matters that consent authority must disregard

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
  • (1) The consent authority must not have regard to—
    • (a) any effect that is outside of the scope of this Act (see section 17):
    • (b) any effect on a person who has given written approval to the application:
    • (c) any adverse effect of the activity on the built environment if the land use plan or a national rule permits an activity with that effect.
  • (2) See also—
    • (a) section 164(2), which provides that if a planning consent application is for an activity that is a restricted discretionary activity, the consent authority may have regard to a matter only if discretion is reserved in relation to that matter by a land use plan, proposed land use plan, national rule, or water services standard; and
    • (b) section 14, which applies to a consent authority when considering a planning consent application.
  • (3) Subsection (1)(b) does not apply if the person withdraws their approval by written notice received by the consent authority before the hearing or, if there is no hearing, before the application is decided.
