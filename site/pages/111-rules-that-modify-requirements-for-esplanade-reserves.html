§111 — Rules that modify requirements for esplanade reserves

PagePA2026provision
  • (1) For allotments of less than 4 hectares created by the subdivision of land, a rule in a land use plan may—
    • (a) specify the required width of any esplanade reserve required under clause 38 of Schedule 7; or
    • (b) provide that no esplanade reserve is required under that clause; or
    • (c) provide that, instead of an esplanade reserve required under that clause, an esplanade strip of a width specified in the rule may be created in accordance with clause 44 of Schedule 7.
  • (2) For allotments of 4 hectares or more created by the subdivision of land, a rule in a land use plan may provide that—
    • (a) an esplanade reserve of a specified width is required under clause 38 of Schedule 7; or
    • (b) an esplanade strip of a specified width must be created under clause 44 of Schedule 7.
  • (3) A rule in a land use plan may—
    • (a) specify the required width of any esplanade reserve required under sec- tion 345(3) of the Local Government Act 1974 (being greater or less than the 20 metres referred to in that section); or
    • (b) provide that section 345(3) of the Local Government Act 1974 does not apply.
