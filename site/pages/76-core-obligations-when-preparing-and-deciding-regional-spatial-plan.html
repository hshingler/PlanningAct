§76 — Core obligations when preparing and deciding regional spatial plan

PagePA2026provisionRSP
  • (1) This section and sections 77 and 78 set out the core obligations that apply to—
    • (a) a spatial plan committee preparing a draft regional spatial plan; and
    • (b) the local authorities of a region when deciding whether to approve a draft regional spatial plan for notification (see clause 17 of Schedule 2); and
    • (c) the local authorities of a region, or the Minister , when deciding a regional spatial plan (see clauses 25 and 27 of Schedule 2).
  • (2) A regional spatial plan must—
    • (a) set out the key drivers of growth and change in the region; and
    • (b) map or otherwise record the broad spatial layout of the region over the next 30 years or longer, including—
    • (i) priority areas for growth and change ; and
      • (ii) existing and future infrastructure that is of strategic importance for growth and change ; and
      • (iii) significant constraints on using or developing—
        • (A) land ; and
        • (B) the coastal marine area ; and
      • (iv) priority areas for planning for adapting to the effects of climate change; and
    • (c) map or otherwise record each of the following in the region:
      • (i) any existing designation that applies in the region, if the project to which the designation relates is significant for the district, region, or nation:
      • (ii) any designation to which clause 37(2) of Schedule 5 applies:
      • (iii) the indicative location of any future designation and details of that future designation (see clause 14 of Schedule 2):
      • (iv) any statutory acknowledgement (see section 81):
      • (v) any site of significance to Māori :
      • (vi) any customary marine title area and any protected customary rights area :
      • (vii) aquaculture settlement areas .
  • (3) A regional spatial plan must—
    • (a) implement relevant national instruments under this Act and relevant national instruments under the Natural Environment Act 2026; and
    • (b) be consistent with any water conservation order that applies in the region; and
    • (c) be prepared in accordance with regulations.
  • (4) The spatial plan committee, local authorities, or Minister (as the case requires) must be satisfied that the regional spatial plan supports—
    • (a) the resolution of potential conflicts between national instruments under this Act and national instruments under the Natural Environment Act 2026; and
    • (b) the alignment of planning under this Act and the Natural Environment Act 2026 with investment decisions and other planning for infrastructure.
  • (5) Subsection (6) applies to a regional spatial plan that—
    • (a) applies to an area that includes, or is directly adjacent to, ngā rohe moana o ngā hapū o Ngāti Porou; or
    • (b) otherwise directly affects ngā rohe moana o ngā hapū o Ngāti Porou.
  • (6) The regional spatial plan must map or otherwise record—
    • (a) ngā rohe moana o ngā hapū o Ngāti Porou; and
    • (b) any customary marine title area (within the meaning of section 9 of the Ngā Rohe Moana o Ngā Hapū o Ngāti Porou Act 2019); and
    • (c) any area to which a protected customary activity applies.
