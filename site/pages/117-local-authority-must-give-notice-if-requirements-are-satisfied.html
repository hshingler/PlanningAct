§117 — Local authority must give notice if requirements are satisfied

PagePA2026provision
  • (1) A local authority must give public notice that future provisions replace tempor- ary provisions in an area if—
    • (a) the local authority is satisfied that the rules described in section 116(1)(a) for that area are met; and
    • (b) all of the following provisions are operative:
    • (i) the rules described in section 116(1)(a):
      • (ii) the future provisions.
  • (2) The notice must—
    • (a) describe the area to which the future provisions apply; and
    • (b) specify the date on and from which the future provisions will apply; and
    • (c) summarise the future provisions and the temporary provisions they replace.
  • (3) A local authority that gives notice must, without using the process in Schedule 3, amend its land use plan to record that on and from the date specified in the notice—
    • (a) the future provisions apply to the area; and
    • (b) the temporary provisions no longer apply to that area.
  • (4) To avoid doubt, there is no right of appeal against, and no right to apply to the Planning Tribunal for review of, a decision of a local authority to give or refuse to give notice under this section.
  • Changing provisions in land use plan without using Schedule 3 process if authorised by planning consent
