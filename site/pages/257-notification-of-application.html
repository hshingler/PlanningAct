§257 — Notification of application

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
    • (1) The applicant for a declaration must serve notice of the application in the pre- scribed form on every person directly affected by the application.
    • (2) The notice must be served within 5 working days after the application is made to the court.
