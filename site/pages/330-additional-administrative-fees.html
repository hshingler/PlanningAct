§330 — Additional administrative fees

PagePA2026provision
  • (1) If a fee fixed under section 329 is, in any particular case, inadequate to enable a local authority to recover its actual and reasonable costs for the matter con- cerned, the local authority may (after considering the criteria in section 331(2) and (3)) require the person who is liable to pay the fee to also pay an additional fee to the local authority.
  • (2) A local authority must, on request by any person liable to pay a fee fixed under section 329, provide an estimate of any additional fee likely to be imposed under this section.
  • (3) A person may apply to the Planning Tribunal to review a decision to require an additional fee under this section.
