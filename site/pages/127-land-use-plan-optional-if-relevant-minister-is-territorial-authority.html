§127 — Land use plan optional if relevant Minister is territorial authority

PagePA2026provision
  • (1) Subsection (2) applies to an area of New Zealand for which the relevant Minis- ter is the territorial authority under section 22(1) of the Local Government Act 2002.
  • (2) The relevant Minister may, but is not required to, prepare a land use plan for the area.
  • (3) In this section, relevant Minister means the Minister of the Crown who, under the authority of a warrant or with the authority of the Prime Minister, is respon- sible for administering the Local Government Act 2002.
