§146 — Consequences of applicant’s failure to respond to requests, etc

PagePA2026provision
  • (1) A consent authority may decide that an application for a planning consent is incomplete if—
    • (a) the applicant was required to provide one of the following responses:
    • (i) to provide further information in response to a request under sec- tion 143 within the time frame prescribed in regulations; or
      • (ii) to tell the consent authority in a written notice whether the appli- cant agrees to the commissioning of a report under section 144 within the time frame prescribed in regulations; or
      • (iii) to pay an administrative fee to the consent authority required under section 329 or 330 and specified in a written notice by an agreed date; or
      • (iv) to give the consent authority written approval for a proposed activity under section 151(1)(b)(i) by an agreed date; and
    • (b) 3 months after the expiry of the applicable time frame specified in para- graph (a), the applicant has not provided the required response; and
    • (c) the consent authority has notified the applicant of its intention to return the application.
  • (2) After deciding that an application is incomplete under this section, the consent authority may return the application to the applicant with written reasons for the decision.
  • (3) If, after an application has been returned as incomplete, that application is lodged again with the consent authority, that application is to be treated as a new application.
  • (4) In this section, agreed date means a date agreed between the applicant and the consent authority.
