§182 — Procedure for appeal

PagePA2026provision
  • (1) Notice of an appeal under section 181 must be in the prescribed form and—
    • (a) state the reasons for the appeal and the relief sought; and
    • (b) state any matters required by regulations; and
    • (c) be lodged with the Environment Court and served on the consent author- ity whose decision is appealed within 15 working days after receiving notice of the decision in accordance with this Act.
  • (2) The appellant must ensure that a copy of the notice of appeal is served on every person referred to in section 181(1) (other than the appellant) within 5 working days after the notice is lodged with the Environment Court.
