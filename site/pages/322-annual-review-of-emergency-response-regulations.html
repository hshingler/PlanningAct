§322 — Annual review of emergency response regulations

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
  • The Minister must, within 12 months after any regulations made under section 320 come into force, and once in every 12 months after that while the regula- tions remain in force,—
    • (a) review the operation and effectiveness of the regulations; and
    • (b) prepare a report on the review; and
    • (c) present the report to the House of Representatives as soon as practicable after it has been completed; and
    • (d) make public the outcomes of the review.
