§241 — Minister may direct local authority to take action

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
    • (1) The Minister may direct a local authority to take any action that the Minister considers necessary to address any issue with the local authority’s exercise or performance of its functions, powers, or duties.
    • (2) However, the Minister must not issue a direction unless—
      • (a) the Minister has investigated the local authority under section 237(1)(a) or (c) in relation to the relevant function, power, or duty; and
      • (b) the Minister has made recommendations to the local authority under sec- tion 237(1)(b) or (d) in relation to the relevant function, power, or duty.
    • (3) The Minister is not required to comply with the requirement in subsection
    • (2) (a) if the Minister has reasonable evidence that a local authority is not exer- cising or performing the relevant functions, powers, or duties under this Act.
    • (4) In subsection (3), reasonable evidence has the meaning given in section 239(4) as if a reference to the territorial authority were a reference to the local authority.
