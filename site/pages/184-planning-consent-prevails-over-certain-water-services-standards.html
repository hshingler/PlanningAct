§184 — Planning consent prevails over certain water services standards

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
  • (1) A planning consent that is granted before one of the following standards is made prevails over that standard:
    • (a) a stormwater environmental performance standard:
    • (b) a wastewater environmental performance standard.
  • (2) However, subsection (1) ceases to apply if—
    • (a) the consent authority reviews the conditions of the planning consent under section 198(1)(c); and
    • (b) the review results in either or both of the standards listed in subsection (1) prevailing over the planning consent.
