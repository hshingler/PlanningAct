§115 — Land use plan may make area subject to future provisions

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
  • (1) A land use plan may provide that an area in a district is subject to—
    • (a) temporary provisions; and
    • (b) future provisions.
  • (2) If an area is subject to temporary and future provisions,—
    • (a) the future provisions apply to the area on and from the date that the terri- torial authority gives notice under section 117 that the relevant rules are met; and
    • (b) the temporary provisions apply to the area until that date.
