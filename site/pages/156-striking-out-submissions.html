§156 — Striking out submissions

PagePA2026provision
Prototype view of your Logseq content. Text and links are extracted from the EDN export; classifications and interpretations have not been independently validated.
  • (1) A consent authority may, in the manner prescribed in regulations, strike out a submission, or part of a submission, if the consent authority considers that the submission or the part discloses no reasonable or relevant case in relation to the application.
  • (2) A person may apply to the Planning Tribunal to review a consent authority’s decision to strike out a submission or part of a submission.
