§77 — Regional spatial plan must include standardised plan provisions as directed by national instrument

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
  • (1) This section applies to a spatial plan committee, the local authorities of a region, and the Minister when performing the functions referred to in section 76(1).
  • (2) The spatial plan committee, local authorities of a region, or Minister, as rele- vant to their decisions,—
    • (a) must include a standardised plan provision in the regional spatial plan if national standards under this Act or the Natural Environment Act 2026 direct that the provision must be included; and
    • (b) may include a standardised plan provision in the regional spatial plan if national standards under this Act or the Natural Environment Act 2026 direct that the provision may be included.
  • (3) The spatial plan committee, local authorities of a region, or Minister, as rele- vant to their decisions, may do any of the following only if authorised by national standards under this Act or the Natural Environment Act 2026:
    • (a) determine where a standardised plan provision applies spatially:
    • (b) select which standardised plan provision, out of 2 or more alternatives set out in the national standards, it will include in the regional spatial plan:
    • (c) determine any content in a standardised plan provision from within parameters set out in the national standards:
    • (d) choose not to include a standardised plan provision.
  • (4) The spatial plan committee, local authorities, and Minister must not amend a standardised plan provision.
