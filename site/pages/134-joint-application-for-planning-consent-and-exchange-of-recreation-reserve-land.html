§134 — Joint application for planning consent and exchange of recreation reserve land

PagePA2026provision

  • (1) A person may make a joint application for a planning consent and an exchange of recreation reserve land under section 15AA of the Reserves Act 1977 if the consent authority—
    • (a) is also the administering body in which the recreation reserve land is vested; and
    • (b) agrees that the applications may be made jointly.
  • (2) If a joint application is made, the application to exchange recreation reserve land must be—
    • (a) processed, with the planning consent application, in accordance with this Part; and
    • (b) decided under section 15AA of the Reserves Act 1977.
