§113 — Methods relating to incentives

PagePA2026provision
  • A method in a land use plan may provide an incentive to a land owner to undertake an activity only if—
    • (a) the local authority considers that the activity will help achieve the objectives and policies of the plan; and
    • (b) the incentive complies with the requirements, if any, prescribed by a national instrument or regulations.
