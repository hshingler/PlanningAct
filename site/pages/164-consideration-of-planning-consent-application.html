§164 — Consideration of planning consent application

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
  • (1) The consent authority must have regard to the following:
    • (a) any adverse effect on—
      • (i) a person, unless section 163(1)(b) applies:
      • (ii) the built environment:
    • (b) any effect that is—
      • (i) positive:
      • (ii) cumulative:
    • (c) any measure proposed or agreed to by the applicant to avoid, remedy, mitigate, offset, or compensate for any adverse effects on a person or the built environment resulting, or likely to result, from the activity:
    • (d) any relevant provisions of a water services standard:
    • (e) any relevant provisions of key instruments in accordance with sections 14 and 15:
    • (f) if the application is affected by section 191, the value of the investment to the existing consent holder:
    • (g) the matters specified in sections 165 to 173.
  • (2) However, if a planning consent application is for an activity that is a restricted discretionary activity, the consent authority may have regard to a matter only if discretion is reserved in relation to that matter by any of the following:
    • (a) a land use plan or proposed land use plan:
    • (b) a national rule:
    • (c) a water services standard.
