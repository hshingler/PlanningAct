§140 — Deferral pending application for additional consents

PagePA2026provision
  • (1) A consent authority may determine not to proceed with the notification or hear- ing of an application for a planning consent if it considers on reasonable grounds that—
    • (a) other planning consents under this Act will also be required in respect of the proposal to which the application relates; and
    • (b) it is appropriate, for the purpose of better understanding the nature of the proposal, that applications for any 1 or more of those other planning consents be made before proceeding further.
  • (2) The consent authority must—
    • (a) make a determination under subsection (1)—
    • (i) before it decides whether to give public or targeted notification, or decides to not notify, an application for a planning consent under section 147; and
      • (ii) before a hearing is held in relation to the application; and
    • (b) notify the applicant of the determination.
  • (3) The determination does not affect whether notification or hearing of the appli- cation is required.
  • (4) The applicant may apply to the Planning Tribunal to review a determination under this section.
