§196 — Cancellation of consent for ORE generation infrastructure activities if

PagePA2026provision
  • permit expires, etc A planning consent for any ORE generation infrastructure activities (excluding decommissioning activities) is cancelled if and when the consent holder ceases to be the permit holder of a current commercial permit granted under the Off- shore Renewable Energy Act 2026 that relates to the area subject to the consent by reason of the expiry or revocation or surrender in full of the commercial permit.
