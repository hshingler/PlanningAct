§188 — Commencement of consent if section 135 applies

PagePA2026provision
  • A planning consent to which section 135 applies does not commence,—
    • (a) in the case of a subdivision consent, until the date that the land to which the consent relates is vested in the consent holder under clause 77 of Schedule 7; or
    • (b) in every other case, until the proposed location of the activity has been reclaimed and a certificate has been issued under clause 73 of Schedule 7 for the reclamation.
