NEA §229 — Functions of regional councils

PageprovisionNEA2026
  • (1) A regional council has the following functions:
    • (a) making and reviewing a natural environment plan for its region:
      NEP
    • (b) administering and implementing its natural environment plan:
      NEP
    • (c) monitoring the implementation of this Act within its region:
    • (d) acting as a permit authority for its region:
    • (e) monitoring and enforcing compliance with this Act:
    • (f) monitoring, within its region,—
      • (i) the state of the natural environment; and
      • (ii) the effects on human health caused by the discharge of contaminants:
    • (g) setting environmental limits for its region:
    • (h) jointly with other local authorities, making and maintaining a regional spatial plan for the region:
      RSP
    • (i) regulating and managing effects on natural resources and people:
    • (j) any other functions or duties specified in this Act or in the Planning Act 2026.
  • (2) Despite section 228, a regional council—
    • (a) must not perform its functions in a manner that is inconsistent with section 12(1); but
    • (b) may perform its functions to control aquaculture activities in the coastal marine area so as to avoid, remedy, or mitigate the effects of aquaculture activities on fishing and fisheries resources.
