§199 — Notice of review

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
    • (1) A notice of intent to review the conditions of a consent under section 198—
      • (a) must advise the consent holder of the conditions of the consent that are the subject of the review; and
      • (b) must state the reasons for the review; and
      • (c) must specify the information that the consent authority took into account in making its decision to review the consent, unless the notice is given under section 198(1)(a) or (2); and
      • (d) must advise a consent holder by whom a fee is payable under the regula- tions in relation to the review—
      • (i) of the fact that the fee is payable; and
        • (ii) of the estimated amount of the fee; and
      • (e) may propose, and invite the consent holder to propose within 20 working days after service of the notice, new consent conditions.
    • (2) If notification of the review is required under section 200, the notification must include a summary of the notice served under this section, and the notification must be served within—
      • (a) 30 working days after the service of the notice (if the consent holder is invited to propose new conditions); or
      • (b) 10 working days after the service of the notice (if the consent holder is not invited to propose new conditions).
