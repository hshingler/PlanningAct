§121 — Joint land use plans

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
  • (1) Two or more local authorities may agree to prepare, implement, and administer a joint land use plan that applies to their combined areas.
  • (2) A joint land use plan—
    • (a) must be prepared in accordance with subpart 2 (including Schedule 3); and
    • (b) must clearly identify which local authority or local authorities are responsible for administering each provision of the joint land use plan.
  • (3) A joint proposed land use plan that is notified for submissions by 1 or more local authorities in accordance with clause 15 or 16 of Schedule 3 must be treated as if it were a separate proposed land use plan for each area of those local authorities.
  • (4) A joint land use plan (or provision in a joint land use plan) that becomes opera- tive in accordance with clause 44 of Schedule 3 in relation to 1 or more local authorities must be treated as if it were a separate land use plan (or a provision in a separate land use plan) for each area of those local authorities.
  • (5) Clauses 30 and 30A of Schedule 7 of the Local Government Act 2002 apply to the appointment and conduct of any joint committee set up for the purposes of preparing, implementing, or administering a joint land use plan.
  • (6) In this section, area,—
    • (a) in relation to a territorial authority, means the district of the territorial authority; and
    • (b) in relation to a regional council, means the coastal marine area that is within the region of the regional council.
