NEA §33 — Meaning of rule

PageprovisionNEA2026
  • (1) A rule means a provision of a key instrument that does any of the following:
    • (a) classifies an activity as a permitted, controlled, restricted discretionary, discretionary, or prohibited activity:
    • (b) specifies requirements for carrying out a permitted activity (for example, requirements referred to in section 39):
    • (c) reserves matters of control for controlled activities:
    • (d) specifies requirements for carrying out a controlled activity, restricted discretionary activity, or discretionary activity:
    • (e) reserves matters of discretion in relation to a restricted discretionary activity:
    • (f) specifies requirements for information to be included in an application for a natural resource permit:
    • (g) requires or precludes targeted or public notification of an application for a natural resource permit for an activity:
    • (h) specifies conditions of a natural resource permit that may be included under section 175 or 178.
  • (2) A rule may—
    • (a) include any provision that is necessary or incidental to the operation of the rule; and
    • (b) in the case of a national rule only, do anything described in section 84(6).
