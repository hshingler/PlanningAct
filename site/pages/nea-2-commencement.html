NEA §2 — Commencement

PageprovisionNEA2026
  • (1) This Act comes into force on the day after Royal assent, with some exceptions.
  • (2) The following provisions come into force on a date or dates set by Order in Council:
    • (a) subpart 2 of Part 2 (provisions relating to duties and restrictions):
    • (b) section 35 (consequences of certain activity classifications):
    • (c) sections 125, 126, and 127(2) (provisions relating to natural environment plans):
    • (d) section 129 (provisions relating to coastal matters set out in Schedule 4) but only for the purpose of giving effect to clauses 2, 3, and 7 to 60 of Schedule 4:
    • (e) section 130 (provisions relating to water conservation orders set out in Schedule 5):
    • (f) section 131 (provisions relating to freshwater farm plans set out in Schedule 6):
    • (g) section 134 (provisions relating to wildlife approvals, including Schedule 3):
    • (h) sections 135 to 217 (provisions relating to natural resource permits, including Schedule 2):
    • (i) sections 255 to 312 (provisions relating to enforcement):
    • (j) sections 313 to 317 (provisions relating to emergency works):
    • (k) section 345 (obligations relating to contaminated land or land used for hazardous activity or industry).
  • (3) Any part of this Act that has not come into force 4 years after Royal assent comes into force then.
  • (4) An Order in Council made under this section is secondary legislation (see Part 3 of the Legislation Act 2019 for publication requirements).
