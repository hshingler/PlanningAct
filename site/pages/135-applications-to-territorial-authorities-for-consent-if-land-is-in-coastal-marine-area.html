§135 — Applications to territorial authorities for consent if land is in coastal marine area

PagePA2026provision
  • (1) If an application for a subdivision consent is made to a territorial authority and any part, or all, of the land proposed to be subdivided is in the coastal marine area, the territorial authority must decide the application as if the whole of that land were part of the district, and the provisions of this Act apply accordingly.
  • (2) Subsection (3) applies if—
    • (a) an application is made to a territorial authority for a planning consent for an activity that an applicant intends to undertake within the district of that authority once the proposed location of the activity has been reclaimed; and
    • (b) on the date that the application is made, the proposed location of the activity is still within the coastal marine area.
  • (3) The authority may hear and decide the application as if the application related to an activity within its district, and the provisions of this Act apply accord- ingly.
  • (4) Section 188 applies to every consent granted under subsection (3).
