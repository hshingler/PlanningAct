§110 — Plan may permit effects that water services standard does not deal with

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
  • (1) Subsection (2) applies if a water services standard provides that—
    • (a) an activity is permitted; or
    • (b) an activity does not require a land use consent.
  • (2) A land use plan may permit, and impose conditions that deal with, any effects of the activity that differ from those regulated or managed in the water services standard.
  • (3) However, subsection (2) does not apply if the water services standard provides otherwise.
