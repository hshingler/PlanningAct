§68 — Withdrawal of proposed national instrument

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
  • (1) The Minister may withdraw all or part of a proposed national instrument at any time before it is made under section 69.
  • (2) The Minister must give public notice of the withdrawal and include reasons for the withdrawal.
