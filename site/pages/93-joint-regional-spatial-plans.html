§93 — Joint regional spatial plans

PagePA2026provision
  • (1) The local authorities of 2 or more regions may agree to prepare, implement, and administer a joint regional spatial plan that applies to their combined regions.
  • (2) A joint regional spatial plan must be prepared in accordance with this subpart (including Schedules 2 and 4), which applies as if references to the local authorities of a region were references to the local authorities of the combined regions.
  • (3) A joint regional spatial plan must clearly identify which local authority or local authorities are responsible for administering each provision of the joint regional spatial plan.
  • (4) A joint regional spatial plan adopted under clause 34 of Schedule 2 by all local authorities of 1 or more regions must be treated as if it were a separate regional spatial plan for each of those regions.
