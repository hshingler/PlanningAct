§85 — Review of implementation plan

PagePA2026provision
  • (1) A spatial plan committee must review and, if necessary, amend its implementa- tion plan—
    • (a) no later than 6 months after adopting a draft regional spatial plan that is a change to a regional spatial plan; and
    • (b) at any other time as required by regulations.
  • (2) A review of, and any amendment to, an implementation plan must be carried out in the manner prescribed by regulations.
