§225 — Meaning of public authority

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
  • In sections 226 to 231, public authority means any of the following:
    • (a) a local authority:
    • (b) a public service agency within the meaning given in section 5 of the Public Service Act 2020:
    • (c) a joint committee appointed under clause 30 of Schedule 7 of the Local Government Act 2002:
    • (d) a local board within the meaning given in section 5(1) of the Local Gov- ernment Act 2002.
