NEA §244 — Content of iwi participation agreements

PageprovisionNEA2026
  • (1) An iwi participation agreement may specify—
    • (a) how 1 or more iwi authorities (including hapū) will participate in processes for iwi authority participation provided for in this Act or the Planning Act 2026 (including processes relating to consultation on regional spatial plans, land use plans, and natural environment plans):
      RSP
    • (b) how 1 or more iwi authorities (including hapū) and the relevant local authorities will engage on any other relevant matters under this Act and the Planning Act 2026 necessary to implement Treaty settlement redress.
  • (2) An iwi participation agreement may include provisions that relate to permitting or consenting processes under this Act or the Planning Act 2026 if the iwi authority (including hapū) is an affected party or a qualifying person or to provide for matters under subsection (1)﻿(b).
  • (3) A local authority may decline to agree to the inclusion of any proposed provisions that it does not wish to be included in the agreement.
  • (4) An iwi participation agreement must not include any matter precluded or not provided for by this section.
  • (5) An iwi participation agreement must—
    • (a) be recorded in writing; and
    • (b) be in the form prescribed by regulations (if any); and
    • (c) identify the relevant iwi authorities (including hapū) and the relevant local authorities; and
    • (d) identify the relevant area to which the agreement relates; and
    • (e) include any other mandatory content required by regulations.
  • (6) An iwi participation agreement must comply with any other requirements of regulations.
