§248 — Collection of data in support of system performance

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
    • (1) The chief executive may collect data for the purposes described in subsec- tion (2) from any person that performs or exercises functions, powers, or duties under this Act or the Natural Environment Act 2026.
    • (2) The purposes for which data may be collected are—
      • (a) to enable the chief executive to perform the system performance func- tion under section 246; or
      • (b) to support any 1 or more of the following:
      • (i) the system performance framework:
        • (ii) digital platforms, systems, and tools, including plans and consent- ing documents:
        • (iii) a system performance report under section 249:
        • (iv) a strategic review under section 250:
      • (v) an independent review under section 251.
    • (3) The chief executive may specify—
      • (a) the form, manner, and time frame in which the person must provide data; and
      • (b) if the same data is to be provided on a regular basis, the frequency of provision of the data.
    • (4) If the chief executive collects data under this section from a local authority, the local authority must provide—
      • (a) the data that it holds on record; and
      • (b) any other data prescribed in regulations.
    • (5) If the chief executive collects data under this section from a person that is not a local authority, the person must provide the data that it holds on record that is prescribed in regulations.
