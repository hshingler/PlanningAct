§224 — Provision of relevant information to post-settlement governance entity

PagePA2026provision
Linked references reflect saved graph links. Unlinked references are candidate title matches and may need review. This prototype is not a verified legal publication.
  • (1) This section applies if—
    • (a) a consent authority is or was required by legislation to provide relevant information relating to a planning consent application for an activity within, adjacent to, or directly affecting a statutory area of a post-settle- ment governance entity; but
    • (b) the requirement no longer applies (for example, because the period spe- cified in the legislation has expired).
  • (2) The consent authority must provide the post-settlement governance entity with a summary of the planning consent application if the application is received by the consent authority.
  • (3) The summary must be the same as would be given to an affected person by tar- geted notification under section 149 or as may be agreed between the post- settlement governance entity and the consent authority.
  • (4) A consent authority must provide the post-settlement governance entity with the summary—
    • (a) as soon as is reasonably practicable after the consent authority receives the planning consent application; and
    • (b) before the consent authority decides under section 147 whether to notify the application.
  • (5) The post-settlement governance entity may, by written notice to the consent authority,—
    • (a) waive the right to be provided with the summary or copy of the notice; and
    • (b) state the scope of that waiver and the period that it applies for.
  • (6) This section does not affect the duty of the consent authority to decide,—
    • (a) under section 147, whether to notify an application:
    • (b) under section 151, whether the post-settlement governance entity is an affected person in relation to an activity.
