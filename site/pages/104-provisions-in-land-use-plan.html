§104 — Provisions in land use plan

PagePA2026provision
  • (1) A land use plan—
    • (a) must include objectives, policies, and rules; and
    • (b) must include a relief framework if required by clause 65 of Schedule 3; and
    • (c) may include methods; and
    • (d) may include designations in accordance with Schedule 5.
  • (2) A land use plan may incorporate material by reference in the manner pre- scribed by regulations.
