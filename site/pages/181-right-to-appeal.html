§181 — Right to appeal

PagePA2026provision
  • (1) Any 1 or more of the following persons may appeal to the Environment Court under section 182 against the whole or any part of a decision of a consent authority on an application for a planning consent, on an application for a change of consent conditions, or on a review of consent conditions:
    • (a) the applicant or consent holder:
    • (b) a person who made a submission on the application or review of consent conditions.
  • (2) A person exercising a right of appeal under subsection (1)(b) may appeal—
    • (a) any matter that was raised in the person’s submission except any part of the submission that is struck out under section 156; and
    • (b) any matter that was not raised in the person’s submission.
  • (3) This section is in addition to the rights provided for in Schedule 10 (which pro- vides for review by the Planning Tribunal).
