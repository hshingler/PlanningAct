§233 — Local authority obligation to notify Minister of decision to negotiate or

PagePA2026provision
  • enter agreement
    • (1) If 1 or more local authorities commence negotiating with 1 or more iwi author- ities (including hapū) with a view to entering into an iwi participation agree- ment, the local authorities must notify the Minister responsible for this Act or the Natural Environment Act 2026 (whichever is relevant) that negotiations have commenced.
    • (2) If 1 or more local authorities decide to enter into an iwi participation agree- ment, they must notify the relevant Minister that they are doing so.
