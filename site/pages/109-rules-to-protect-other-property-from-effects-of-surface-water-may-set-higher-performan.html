§109 — Rules to protect other property from effects of surface water may set higher performance criteria than building code

PagePA2026provision
  • (1) A rule in a land use plan may, for the purpose of protecting other property from the effects of surface water, include requirements that—
    • (a) apply to a person undertaking building work; and
    • (b) set performance criteria that are in addition to, or more restrictive than, the building code.
  • (2) This section applies despite section 17(1)(a) and (j).
  • (3) In this section, building code and other property have the meanings given in section 7 of the Building Act 2004.
