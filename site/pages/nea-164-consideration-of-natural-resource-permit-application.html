NEA §164 — Consideration of natural resource permit application

PageprovisionNEA2026
  • (1) The permit authority must have regard to the following:
    • (a) any adverse effect on—
      • (i) a person, unless section 163(1)﻿(a) applies:
      • (ii) natural resources:
    • (b) any effect that is—
      • (i) positive:
      • (ii) cumulative:
    • (c) any measure proposed or agreed to by the applicant to avoid, remedy, mitigate, offset, or compensate for any adverse effects on natural resources or people resulting, or likely to result, from the activity:
    • (d) any relevant provisions of a water services standard:
    • (e) any relevant provisions of key instruments in accordance with sections 14 and 15:
    • (f) any relevant matter specified in an action plan:
    • (g) if the application is affected by section 195(a) (which applies section 191 of the Planning Act 2026), the value of the investment to the existing permit holder:
    • (h) if the application is affected by section 195(a) (which applies section 191 of the Planning Act 2026) in relation to long-lived infrastructure, the effects of that infrastructure:
    • (i) the matters specified in sections 165 to 172.
  • (2) Subsection (1) is subject to subsections (3) to (5).
  • (3) If the activity is a natural resource use activity, the permit authority may, in its discretion, consider any adverse effect of the activity on natural resources or people regardless of whether the natural environment plan or a national rule permits an activity with that effect.
    NEP
  • (4) If a natural resource permit application is for an activity that is a restricted discretionary activity, the permit authority may have regard to a matter only if discretion is reserved in relation to that matter by any of the following:
    • (a) a natural environment plan or proposed natural environment plan:
      NEP
    • (b) a national rule:
    • (c) a water services standard.
  • (5) If a natural resource permit application is for an activity that is a controlled activity, the permit authority may have regard to a matter only if control is reserved in relation to that matter by any of the following:
    • (a) a natural environment plan or proposed natural environment plan:
      NEP
    • (b) a national rule:
    • (c) a water services standard.
