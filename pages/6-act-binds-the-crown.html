§6 — Act binds the Crown

PagePA2026provision
Prototype view of your Logseq content. Text and links are extracted from the EDN export; classifications and interpretations have not been independently validated.
  • (1) This Act binds the Crown except as provided in this section and section 311.
  • (2) This Act does not apply to any work or activity of the Crown that—
    • (a) is a use of land within the meaning given in section 21; and
    • (b) the Minister of Defence certifies is necessary for reasons of national security.
  • (3) Section 21(1) does not apply to any work or activity of the Crown within the boundaries of any area of land held or managed under the Conservation Act 1987 or any other Act specified in Schedule 1 of that Act (other than land held for administrative purposes) that—
    • (a) is consistent with a conservation management strategy, conservation management plan, or management plan established under the Conserva- tion Act 1987 or any other Act specified in Schedule 1 of that Act; and
    • (b) does not have a significant adverse effect beyond the boundary of the area of land.
  • (4) Section 21 does not apply to the detention of prisoners in a court cell block that is declared by notice in the Gazette to be a part of a corrections prison.
