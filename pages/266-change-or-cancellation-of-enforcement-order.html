§266 — Change or cancellation of enforcement order

PagePA2026provision
Prototype view of your Logseq content. Text and links are extracted from the EDN export; classifications and interpretations have not been independently validated.
    • (1) Without limiting section 265(6), any person directly affected by an enforce- ment order may at any time apply to the Environment Court in the prescribed form to change or cancel the order.
    • (2) Sections 262 to 264 (which relate to notification, hearing, and decision-mak- ing) apply to every application under subsection (1) as if it were an application for an enforcement order.
    • (3) No person may apply to change or cancel an order that revokes a planning con- sent on the grounds set out in section 259(1)(f).
