§174 — Decision on planning consent

PagePA2026provision

  • (1) After considering an application for a planning consent, a consent authority may grant or refuse to grant a planning consent.
  • (2) If it grants a planning consent, the consent authority may impose conditions on the planning consent,—
    • (a) for a restricted discretionary activity, only for those matters over which a land use plan, proposed land use plan, water services standard, or national rule has reserved discretion:
    • (b) for a discretionary activity, under section 178.
  • (3) A consent authority may grant a planning consent on the basis that the activity is classified as a restricted discretionary activity or a discretionary activity regardless of what type of activity the application was expressed to be for.
  • (4) A consent authority may refuse to grant a planning consent on the ground that it has inadequate information to decide the application.
  • (5) In making an assessment on the adequacy of the information, the consent authority must have regard to whether any request made of the applicant for further information or reports resulted in further information or any reports being made available.
