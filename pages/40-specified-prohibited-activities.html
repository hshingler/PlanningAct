§40 — Specified prohibited activities

PagePA2026provision
Prototype view of your Logseq content. Text and links are extracted from the EDN export; classifications and interpretations have not been independently validated.
  • (1) The following activities are prohibited activities:
    • (a) prospecting, exploring, or mining for Crown-owned minerals in the internal waters of the Coromandel Peninsula:
    • (b) mining of which the main purpose is to mine mercury.
  • (2) Subsection (1)(a) does not apply to prospecting, exploring, or mining activities set out in section 61(1A) of the Crown Minerals Act 1991.
