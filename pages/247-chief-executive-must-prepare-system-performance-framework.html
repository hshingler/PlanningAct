§247 — Chief executive must prepare system performance framework

PagePA2026provision

    • (1) The chief executive must prepare a system performance framework within 12 months after this Act receives Royal assent.
    • (2) The framework must set out—
      • (a) system performance indicators; and
      • (b) the holders of the information that the chief executive will use to inform their understanding of the indicators; and
      • (c) matters to be addressed in system performance reports; and
      • (d) the process for developing system performance reports, strategic reviews, and independent reviews under sections 249 to 251; and
      • (e) publication requirements for strategic review reports, including the rea- sons that the chief executive may withhold publication of a strategic review report; and
      • (f) any other matters that the chief executive considers relevant or neces- sary.
    • (3) The framework must specify who, and how, the chief executive will consult when—
      • (a) preparing system performance reports; and
      • (b) undertaking strategic reviews; and
      • (c) preparing amendments to the framework.
    • (4) The chief executive must—
      • (a) maintain the framework; and
      • (b) publish the framework on an internet site.
