§74 — Every region must have 1 regional spatial plan

PagePA2026provision
Prototype view of your Logseq content. Text and links are extracted from the EDN export; classifications and interpretations have not been independently validated.
  • (1) There must at all times be 1 regional spatial plan for each region.
  • (2) A regional spatial plan must apply to the entire region but may focus in greater detail on certain parts of the region.
