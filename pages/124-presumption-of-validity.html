§124 — Presumption of validity

PagePA2026provision

  • If a local authority asserts that a land use plan or a provision in a land use plan is operative, the plan or provision—
    • (a) must be treated as having been prepared and approved in accordance with Schedule 3; and
    • (b) must not be challenged except by an enforcement order of a kind speci- fied in section 259(1)(g) (see section 261(3) for application require- ments).
