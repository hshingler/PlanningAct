§130 — Meaning of planning consent

PagePA2026provision
Prototype view of your Logseq content. Text and links are extracted from the EDN export; classifications and interpretations have not been independently validated.
  • In this Act, a planning consent or consent means either of the following:
    • (a) a land use consent, which is a consent to use land or to carry out an activity in, on, under, or over the coastal marine area in a manner that would otherwise contravene section 21 or 22:
    • (b) a subdivision consent, which is a consent to subdivide land in a manner that would otherwise contravene section 23.
