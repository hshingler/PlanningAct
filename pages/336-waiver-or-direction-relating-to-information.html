§336 — Waiver or direction relating to information

PagePA2026provision
Prototype view of your Logseq content. Text and links are extracted from the EDN export; classifications and interpretations have not been independently validated.
  • (1) This section applies if—
    • (a) a person is required to provide information under this Act; and
    • (b) the information is not provided, or is inaccurate, or an associated proced- ural requirement is not complied with.
  • (2) The consent authority or local authority may—
    • (a) waive compliance with the requirement; or
    • (b) direct that the omission or inaccuracy be rectified on terms that the con- sent authority or local authority thinks fit.
