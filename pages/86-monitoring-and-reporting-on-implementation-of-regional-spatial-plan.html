§86 — Monitoring and reporting on implementation of regional spatial plan

PagePA2026provision

  • (1) A spatial plan committee must monitor and report annually on progress in implementing the regional spatial plan, as measured against the implementation plan.
  • (2) Monitoring and reporting must be carried out in the manner prescribed by regu- lations.
