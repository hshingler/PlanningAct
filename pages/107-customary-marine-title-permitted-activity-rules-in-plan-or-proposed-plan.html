§107 — Customary marine title permitted activity rules in plan or proposed plan

  • (1) A land use plan or a proposed land use plan must not include a rule that classi- fies an activity in the common marine and coastal area as a permitted activity unless, before including the rule, the local authority considers whether the activity (as permitted by the rule) will or is likely to have—
    • (a) an adverse effect that is more than minor on the relationship of a custom- ary marine title group with their customary marine title area; or
    • (b) an adverse effect that is more than minor on the relationship of an appli- cant group with the area for which they seek customary marine title.
  • (2) If the local authority considers that the activity will or is likely to have an adverse effect described in subsection (1)(a) or (b), the regional council must identify in the plan or proposed plan that the rule is a customary marine title permitted activity rule.
  • (3) This section applies whether or not a national instrument requires that the per- mitted activity rule be included in the plan or proposed plan.
  • (4) See also sections 66 and 68 of the Marine and Coastal Area (Takutai Moana) Act 2011, which provide that a customary marine title group has an NEP per- mission right in relation to an activity permitted by a customary marine title permitted activity rule in their customary marine title area.
  • (5) In this section and section 108, applicant group has the meaning given in sec- tion 9(1) of the Marine and Coastal Area (Takutai Moana) Act 2011.
