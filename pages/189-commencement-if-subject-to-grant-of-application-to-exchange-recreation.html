§189 — Commencement if subject to grant of application to exchange recreation

  • reserve land If a planning consent is subject to the grant of an application to exchange recre- ation reserve land under section 15AA of the Reserves Act 1977,—
    • (a) the consent authority must notify the applicant when the procedures in sections 15 and 15AA of that Act are complete; and
    • (b) the planning consent commences on—
    • (i) the date of the notification under paragraph (a); or
      • (ii) any later date that is specified in the notification.
