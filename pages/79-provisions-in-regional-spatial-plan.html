§79 — Provisions in regional spatial plan

PagePA2026provision
Prototype view of your Logseq content. Text and links are extracted from the EDN export; classifications and interpretations have not been independently validated.
  • A regional spatial plan—
    • (a) must include objectives; and
    • (b) must include maps; and
    • (c) must include actions; and
    • (d) may include policies, methods, or other types of provisions; but
    • (e) must not include rules.
