§102 — Plan may include bespoke plan provisions if authorised or not precluded by a national instrument

PagePA2026provision

  • A local authority may include a bespoke plan provision in a land use plan or proposed land use plan only if—
    • (a) a national instrument authorises the local authority to prepare a bespoke plan provision (see section 62(2)(b)); or
    • (b) the national instruments do not preclude the local authority from includ- ing a bespoke plan provision.
