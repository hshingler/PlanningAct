§105 — Plan must not permit activity that has certain effects on protected customary rights

  • (1) A land use plan or a proposed land use plan must not include a rule that classi- fies an activity as a permitted activity if that activity will, or is likely to, have an adverse effect that is more than minor on—
    • (a) a protected customary right carried out under Part 3 of the Marine and Coastal Area (Takutai Moana) Act 2011; or
    • (b) a protected customary activity carried out under Part 2 of the Ngā Rohe Moana o Ngā Hapū o Ngāti Porou Act 2019.
  • (2) This section applies despite anything to the contrary in a national instrument.
