§75 — How regional spatial plan is prepared or changed

  • (1) Schedule 2 sets out provisions relating to the preparation and change of regional spatial plans and has effect according to its terms.
  • (2) Schedule 4 sets out provisions relating to the appointment, powers, duties, and funding of independent hearings panels, which have functions to hold the hear- ing and make recommendations when a regional spatial plan is being prepared or changed. Schedule 4 has effect according to its terms.
