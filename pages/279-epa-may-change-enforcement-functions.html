§279 — EPA may change enforcement functions

PagePA2026provision
Prototype view of your Logseq content. Text and links are extracted from the EDN export; classifications and interpretations have not been independently validated.
    • (1) The EPA may change its enforcement function in relation to an incident to another function described in section 277 if the EPA considers that the circum- stances require it.
    • (2) If the EPA decides to change to an intervention function described in section 277(c), it must include its reasons for the change in the notice required under section 278(1)(a).
